OUR SERVICES

Care that begins
with your needs.

All services
01

Therapy

A tooth that reacts to cold, hurts when chewing or develops a dark spot deserves an examination. General dental treatment identifies the cause and considers ways to preserve the tooth, from a filling to root canal treatment.

Service illustration
02

Dental hygiene

Professional hygiene removes tartar and deposits that daily brushing cannot fully manage. It is also a chance to assess your gums and choose a home-care routine suited to your teeth and restorations.

Service illustration
03

Dental implants

A dental implant is one option for replacing a missing tooth. It is placed in the jawbone and supports a crown, bridge or denture. Start with a consultation and a plan that includes both surgery and the final restoration.

Service illustration
04

Prosthetic services

Crowns, bridges and removable dentures restore damaged or missing teeth. A consultation should consider chewing, speech, supporting teeth and everyday cleaning alongside the appearance of your smile.

Service illustration
05

Dental surgery

Oral surgery is considered when a filling or other conservative treatment cannot solve the problem. Common concerns include a badly damaged tooth or a troublesome wisdom tooth. The first decision is whether extraction is indicated and whether the tooth can be retained.

Service illustration
06

Pediatric dentistry

Meeting the dentist helps a child become familiar with a new setting and gives parents practical guidance. A preventive visit allows time to discuss habits and tooth development before pain starts.

Service illustration
07

Teeth whitening

Whitening changes the colour of natural teeth. Start with an examination so the dentist can assess enamel, fillings and gums, discuss your preferred shade and explain realistic outcomes.
